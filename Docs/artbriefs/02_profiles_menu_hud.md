## 1. Character Briefs

### 1.1 Alice Liddell — The Protagonist

**Role:** Player character  
**Subtitle on sheet:** "A Protagonista"

**Quote (sheet):** *"Se este não é meu mundo, então eu o refaço."*

**Silhouette:**
- Mid-length A-line gothic lolita dress with heavy ruffle tiers at hem.
- Fitted corseted bodice, puff-shouldered long sleeves.
- Knee-high black lace-top stockings with visible horizontal stripes.
- Heavy-soled black boots reaching mid-calf.
- Layered pearl/black choker necklace with a central pendant.
- Long, loose dark-brown/near-black wavy hair, typically past shoulder-blades.
- Slight frame — looks young and fragile; contradicted by combat posture.

**Color Palette (from sheet "Paleta de Cores"):**

| Swatch Label | Approx Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Paleta Base (dress cream/ivory) | `#D9CBBF` | Dress underlayer, ruffle faces |

**Key Visual Details:**
- Dress bodice has intricate floral/vine brocade in dark gold thread over navy.
- Ruffle tiers show gradient from cream near waist to darker dusty-gold at hem — as if stained.
- Belt is wide, dark leather, cinched with visible lacing.
- Choker has a heart-shaped or teardrop dark-gemstone pendant.
- Expression range: Serena (calm), Triste (sad), Assustada (scared), Determinada (determined), Quebrada (broken), Esperançosa (hopeful) — six portrait states for narrative triggers.

**Concept Color Variants (sheet "Conceito Inicial"):**

2. Rust/brown — warmer palette, burnt umber tones.

### 3.2 Full Weapon Lineup (armas.png — 5 Weapons)

Each weapon has two forms (normal + transformed "Modo Transformado") and two special abilities shown as in-game action thumbnails.

**Boss Base:** Gato Cheshire

**Transformed:** Blade splits into two curved fragments — chained, whip-like reach.  
**Colors:** Deep violet `#5A1A8C`, purple glow `#9B55D4`, black metal `#1A1020`.  
**Special Abilities:** Invisible Step (blink/dodge), Smile Beyond Beauty (AoE fear pulse).  

#### Weapon 3 — Bengala do Chá Eterno
**Boss Base:** Chapeleiro Maluco (Mad Hatter)  
**Form:** Walking cane with ornate top-hat finial; the cane conceals a scythe blade that deploys.  
**Transformed:** Top-hat opens revealing a massive spinning scythe head.  
**Colors:** Burgundy `#6B1A20`, gold trim `#A07820`, dark wood `#2C1A0A`.  
**Special Abilities:** Madness Roulette (random buff/debuff spin), Tea Party Collapse (AoE slam).  

#### Weapon 4 — Foice da Lagarta Azul
**Boss Base:** Lagarta Azul (Blue Caterpillar)  
**Form:** Traditional scythe with translucent blue crystal blade — smoke/vapor trailing from edge.  
**Transformed:** Blade expands to double length, crystal becomes solid luminous blue.  
**Colors:** Crystal blue `#4080C8`, deep blue smoke `#1A3060`, silver shaft `#808898`.

#### Weapon 5 — Guilhotine Heartbreaker
**Boss Base:** Rainha de Copas  
**Form:** Giant ornate guillotine blade on chains — dragged or swung overhead. Heart motif on blade spine.  
**Transformed:** Chains extend, blade splits, becomes a dual-head flail.  
**Colors:** Deep crimson `#8B1A2C`, tarnished gold `#8C6A2A`, black chain `#1A1010`.  
**Special Abilities:** Execution (instant-kill threshold finisher), Off With Their Heads (decapitation AoE burst).  

**Shared Design Language:** All weapons show visible wear and Wonderland-specific iconography (suits, clocks, mushrooms, cards). All transformed forms add particle FX corresponding to their boss's color. Blade edges in base form are matte; transformed adds emissive glow.

## 4. Main Menu Spec (menu.png)

### 4.1 Overall Composition

**Resolution observed:** Widescreen (approximately 16:9).  
**Background:** Entirely black — no visible scene geometry at neutral state.  
**Central FX:** The Cheshire Cat's face rendered as glowing teal/cyan light points — only the eyes (two small white-teal glowing dots, slightly asymmetric) and the grin (thin curved line of faint teal-white light) are visible against pure black. No body, no face fill — just the floating expression. The grin is positioned center-upper, eyes slightly above midpoint.

**Title Treatment:**
- Two-line stack, center-bottom zone.
- Line 1: `PROJECT` — small, tracked-out caps, white, serif or thin-weight display font. Approx 60–80% of line 2 width.
- Line 2: `ALICE` — large, dominant, white, same font family but bold/display weight. Approximately 3× the cap-height of line 1.
- No subtitle or tagline visible at menu level.
- No decorative frame or border around the title.
- Title sits in the lower 25% of the screen.

**Quote / Flavor Text:**
- Positioned center-screen, roughly at vertical midpoint.
- Italic serif font, off-white (`#E0DDD8`), small body size.
- Text reads: *"Ou talvez... nunca tenha saído."*
- Appears to be randomly selected flavor text (quote attribution: "Cheshire" shown below in smaller text).
- Centered horizontally, not associated with any button.

### 4.2 Button List & Order

All buttons aligned to the **left edge**, approximately 10–12% from screen left, vertically centered in the left column. Small font, white, no decorative background — pure text links over the dark BG.

| # | Button Label | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Novo Jogo | First/top item |
| 2 | Continuar | Second |
| 3 | Configurações | Third (likely leads to Settings submenu) |
| 4 | Sair | Fourth/bottom — Exit game |

**Button styling:**
- No icons, no borders, no button-box background.
- Plain white text, left-aligned.
- Selected state: likely uses color shift or subtle glow (not visible in static screenshot — implement as `#9B55D4` highlight or white with increased brightness).
- Hover/selected item may show the Cheshire grin animating toward it.
- Spacing: approximately equal line-height gaps between items.
- Font: appears to be a clean sans-serif or thin serif — matches the overall gothic-minimal aesthetic.

### 4.3 Color & Mood

| Element | Color |
|---|---|
| Background | `#000000` (pure black) |
| Cheshire eyes glow | `#40D0C8` (teal-cyan) |
| Cheshire grin glow | `#30B0A8` (slightly dimmer teal) |
| Button text (idle) | `#E8E6E2` (near-white) |
| Quote text | `#D4D0C8` (warm off-white) |
| Title "PROJECT" | `#F0EEE8` (white) |
| Title "ALICE" | `#FFFFFF` (pure white) |

**Mood:** Oppressive silence, deep dark, the sense of being watched by something unseen. The Cheshire face is the only "life" in the scene — barely visible, suggesting it could vanish at any moment. No music specified in art but sonic suggestion: low ambient drone, occasional whisper.

### 4.4 UMG Rebuild Notes

- Root: `WBP_MainMenu`
- Canvas Panel, full screen (1920×1080 anchored).
- Background: solid black `UImage` fill, then overlay a `UParticleSystemComponent` or Niagara system for the Cheshire face — rendered as emissive point-light sprites (eyes) + spline-arc (grin).
- Quote text: `URichTextBlock`, italic, centered, `Y=42%` from top.
- Button column: `UVerticalBox` anchored left-center, `X=10%`, `Y=45%`, with `UButton` children using transparent background and text-only styling.
- Title block: `UVerticalBox` bottom-center anchored, `Y=78%` — `UTextBlock` "PROJECT" (small caps) stacked above `UTextBlock` "ALICE" (display large).
- Animate: Cheshire face elements fade in on BeginPlay with a 2–3 second ease-in. Buttons fade in after Cheshire settles. Quote fades in last.

## 5. HUD Spec (lidia-boss.png — Boss Fight UI)

*Note: The lidia-boss.png image is a character concept/key-art portrait, not a gameplay HUD screenshot. The following HUD spec is derived from genre conventions (soulslike), the visual language established in the game's art, and the information visible in the boss fight context of the sheet. Where elements are inferred, they are marked [INFERRED].*

### 5.1 Boss Health Bar

**Style:** Segmented health bar [INFERRED — standard for soulslike boss fights with phase transitions].

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Position | Bottom-center of screen, above center-bottom edge (~8% from bottom) |
| Width | ~60% of screen width, centered |
| Segments | 2 segments (two health phases — Innocent → Boss, or Phase 1 → Phase 2) |
| Segment divider | Hard black break line with small skull or heart-suit icon at division point |
| Fill color Phase 1 | `#C82828` (saturated red) |

| Background track | `#1A0A0A` (near-black) |
| Border | Thin gold filigree frame `#8C6A2A` |
| Depletion FX | Segments drain right-to-left; ghost trail in lighter red lingers 0.5s behind drain |

**Boss Name Display:**
- Above the health bar.
- Large display font: "LÍDIA LIDDELL" in small-caps white.
- Below it, smaller italic: *"Rainha do Coração Partido"* in off-white.
- Phase label appears on segment transition: "FASE II — O CORAÇÃO PARTIDO" centered flash.

### 5.2 Player HP & Stamina

| Bar | Position | Color | Style |

- HP label: small "HP" text or heart icon left of bar.
- Stamina label: small running-figure icon or "ST" text [INFERRED from genre].
