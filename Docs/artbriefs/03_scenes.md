## CENA 01 — O Rio (The Riverbank)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Open riverbank at dusk; dark field, stone outcrops, willow silhouettes |
| **Mood** | Melancholic calm before rupture — bittersweet, intimate |
| **Dominant Palette** | Deep amber `#C45A1A`, dusty indigo `#2A2340`, cobalt blue wildflowers `#1E3A8A`, dark water `#0D1520` |
| **Camera / Composition** | Wide establishing shot of two sisters facing each other across the water; cuts to medium shot of Irma reaching toward Alice; final pull-back wide as Alice flinches away |
| **Key Story Beat** | Irma warns Alice — *"Alice… você está ouvindo?"* Alice looks distant. The last moment of the real world before descent. |
| **Notable VFX** | Subtle blue wildflower petals lifting in a lazy breeze; distant warm fog on the horizon; god-rays through willow canopy; sky transitions from amber sunset to ominous purple overcast across the cut sequence |

| **Mood** | Mounting dread, urgency, uncanny — heartbeat tension |

| **Key Story Beat** | Alice spots the White Rabbit and begins to follow. The watch ticks. The chase begins. Orchestral tension swells. |

## CENA 17 — Final Cinemático (The Reunion)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Liminal light space — between worlds, soft white mist, dissolving Wonderland geometry |
| **Mood** | Cathartic release — the entire game's emotional weight exhaled in a single embrace |
| **Dominant Palette** | Luminous silver-white `#E8EEF5`, soft lavender `#C4B8D8`, pale gold `#D4C890`, ghost blue `#8FA8CC` |

| **Key Story Beat** | Reunion. *"Você finalmente voltou…"* Lídia is freed from the Queen's corruption. Alice wakes. The darkness dissolves. The story closes. |
| **Notable VFX** | White light bloom engulfs the frame gradually; Wonderland geometry (gears, cards, forest) dissolves into light particles around them; soft petal shower in white/silver; depth-of-field blur increases to near-white as the embrace holds; Lídia's corruption tattoos fade from her skin in real time |
